# T1D Treatment Weekly Report - 2026-10-02

**Scope and caveats.** Prior report: baseline of 2026-06-24 (no weekly reports exist between then and now, so this run also flags items from roughly the last 3 months). Sourcing limits this run: web search worked, but direct page fetches of several primary sources (Vertex IR, Yahoo Finance, Diatribe, Manila Times) were blocked by the network proxy, so details below come from search-result summaries and are labelled where primary confirmation is missing. Nothing here is a clinical recommendation.

## Top items this week

### 1. Promotional "diabetes reversal" claim from Shenzhen — treat with caution (Asia)
On 2026-09-28 a GlobeNewswire press release announced an "international medical approach to reversing diabetes" at the Center for Integrative Medicine for Chronic Diseases / Center for Natural Diabetes Therapy at Shenzhen Hengsheng Hospital (described as a Fosun Health subsidiary). It claims 150 T1D patients achieved "remission", of whom 30 stopped exogenous insulin and 120 reduced dose, using integrated traditional Chinese and Western medicine; the release is marketed to international private-pay patients. No peer-reviewed data, trial registration, or definition of "remission" was found. T1D is an autoimmune loss of beta cells and insulin discontinuation without cell replacement or immune intervention would be extraordinary; we regard this as an unverified commercial claim and flag it so followers are not misled. Source: [GlobeNewswire, 2026-09-28](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/28/3369540/0/en/a-new-international-medical-approach-aimed-at-reversing-diabetes-has-officially-launched-in-shenzhen-china.html).

### 2. Encellin partners with Breakthrough T1D on encapsulated islet therapy (Global)
Encellin announced (reported 2026-09-22) a partnership with Breakthrough T1D through its Industry Discovery and Development Partnership program to advance ENCRT-103-hPI, a next-generation encapsulation implant for human islets, with a planned study in Canada. It builds on a completed first-in-human Phase 1 (NCT06408311) that reported non-fibrotic tissue integration, vascularization and viable human islets in retrieved ENC-201 implants. This matters because encapsulation is one route to cell replacement without immunosuppression, the main barrier to wider use. Efficacy (insulin production in people) has not been shown yet. Source: [Encellin/BusinessWire via FinancialContent, 2026-09-22](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-9-22-encellin-partners-with-breakthrough-t1d-to-advance-immune-protected-cell-therapy-for-type-1-diabetes).

### 3. Device and insulin regulatory items (Global)
- Inhaled insulin Afrezza reportedly approved by FDA for children and adolescents (INHALE-1, 26-week Phase 3); exact approval date not confirmed in search results. Source: [Contemporary Pediatrics](https://contemporarypediatrics.com/view/fda-approves-inhaled-insulin-afrezza-for-children-and-adolescents-with-diabetes).
- DreaMed's MODI received FDA 510(k) clearance (Sept 2026) for CGM-driven insulin dosing support for people with **type 2** diabetes on basal plus mealtime injections — not a T1D item but relevant to the decision-support space. Source: [MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2026/09/dreamed-wins-fda-clearance-for-cgm-driven-insulin-automation-tool-for-both-type-2-injection-regimens/).
- A once-weekly basal insulin (Onswik) was reported FDA-approved for adults with type 2 diabetes; no T1D indication. Source: [Beyond Type 1](https://beyondtype1.org/onswik-fda-approval-2026).
- FDA approved finerenone (Kerendia) for CKD associated with T1D (per a search summary; primary source not confirmed).

### 4. Access and policy
The INSULIN Act of 2026 was introduced in the US Senate, capping out-of-pocket costs at $35 or 25% of negotiated price for at least one insulin of each type and dosage form (ADA statement). It is a bill only; passage is uncertain. Source: [ADA](https://diabetes.org/newsroom/press-releases/american-diabetes-associations-statement-introduction-insulin-act-2026).

## Cell & regenerative therapies — status of tracked programs
- **Vertex zimislecel (VX-880).** Per Vertex materials, 12 patients in the FORWARD trial all showed restored endogenous insulin secretion; 10 of 12 were insulin-free, with mean exogenous insulin reduced 92%. Global regulatory submissions (FDA/EMA/MHRA) were guided for 2026; the program holds RMAT, Fast Track, PRIME and ILAP designations. **We could not confirm whether a submission has occurred as of 2026-10-02** (investor pages blocked) — this is the most important item to verify next week. Vertex's VX-264 (encapsulated) was discontinued earlier. Sources: [CGTLive](https://www.cgtlive.com/view/vertex-islet-cell-therapy-vx-880-restores-endogenous-insulin-secretion-type-1-diabetes), [Vertex Q2 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000875320/000087532026000256/ex-991_q22026.htm).
- **Sana Biotechnology UP421 (hypoimmune primary islets, no immunosuppression).** Continued positive results through 14 months (announced 2026-03-13): C-peptide maintained; SC451 (stem-cell-derived, hypoimmune) aimed for a Phase 1 start in 2026. Single patient. No newer update found this run. Source: [GlobeNewswire, 2026-03-13](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/13/3255502/0/en/sana-biotechnology-announces-continued-positive-clinical-results-through-14-months-from-type-1-diabetes-study-of-islet-cell-transplantation-without-immunosuppression.html).

## Immunotherapy & disease delay
Eli Lilly launched two Phase 3 baricitinib trials (announced March 2026): BARICADE-DELAY (stage 1–2 T1D) and BARICADE-PRESERVE (new-onset stage 3a), following a Breakthrough T1D-funded Phase 2. Teplizumab: PROTECT (NEJM) in newly diagnosed youth, βETA PRESERVE Phase 3 ongoing, plus real-world progression data. No new readouts found this week. Sources: [Breakthrough T1D](https://www.breakthrought1d.org/news-and-updates/two-new-trials-baricitinib-to-delay-t1d/), [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03875729](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03875729).

## Asia & Emerging Research Centers
**Searched:** China T1D research; Deng Hongkui chemical reprogramming; Peking University; Shanghai/Beijing/Shenzhen trials; Japan iPSC islet therapy; NMPA registrations. **Status of key programs:**
- **Deng Hongkui / Peking University (CiPSC islets).** No new trial result found this week. Reference: 2024 Cell paper on autologous chemically induced iPSC-islet transplant, one woman insulin-independent beyond one year; Deng received the 2024 Future Science Prize. Source: [Cell 2024](https://www.cell.com/cell/pdf/S0092-8674(24)01022-5.pdf).
- **Shanghai CEMCS (CAS) / Changzheng Hospital — E-islet.** Reported (CAS, 2026-03-18) in *Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology*: three T1D patients (incl. a 15-year-old) treated with endoderm stem cell-derived islet-like tissue, culture time cut from ~40 to 14 days. Very small, uncontrolled series. Source: [CAS](https://english.cas.cn/newsroom/cas-in-media/202603/t20260318_1152861.shtml).
- **China allogeneic iPSC islets (CRG-002).** An exploratory trial for hypoglycemia unawareness is registered (NCT07503028); sponsor/status not verified. Source: [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07503028).
- **Japan — Kyoto University Hospital / Orizuru OZTx-410.** First allogeneic iPSC-derived islet cell sheet transplant in T1D performed Feb 2025; reported HbA1c 7.6% to <5.7% and time in range 43% to >98% within a year in the first patient; two more participants enrolled; availability hoped for in the 2030s. No new update this week. Source: [Kyoto University Hospital](https://diabendonutri.kuhp.kyoto-u.ac.jp/contents/images/2025/04/iPIC_20250414_en_ver2.pdf).
- **NMPA:** no formal IND approvals identified in search results.

## Главное за неделю

### 1. Рекламное заявление о «обращении диабета вспять» из Шэньчжэня — относиться с осторожностью (Азия)
28.09.2026 в пресс-релизе GlobeNewswire объявлен «международный медицинский подход к обращению диабета вспять» в Центре интегративной медицины хронических заболеваний / Центре естественной терапии диабета больницы Shenzhen Hengsheng (описана как дочерняя структура Fosun Health). Заявлено, что у 150 пациентов с СД1 достигнута «ремиссия»: 30 прекратили экзогенный инсулин, 120 снизили дозу; применяется сочетание традиционной китайской и западной медицины; услуга ориентирована на иностранных пациентов, оплачивающих лечение самостоятельно. Рецензируемых данных, регистрации исследования и определения «ремиссии» не найдено. СД1 — аутоиммунная потеря бета-клеток, и отказ от инсулина без замещения клеток или иммунного вмешательства был бы исключительным результатом; мы расцениваем это как непроверенное коммерческое заявление и отмечаем это, чтобы не вводить читателей в заблуждение. Источник: [GlobeNewswire, 28.09.2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/28/3369540/0/en/a-new-international-medical-approach-aimed-at-reversing-diabetes-has-officially-launched-in-shenzhen-china.html).

### 2. Encellin и Breakthrough T1D: партнёрство по инкапсулированной терапии островками (мир)
Компания Encellin объявила (сообщение от 22.09.2026) о партнёрстве с Breakthrough T1D в рамках программы IDDP для развития ENCRT-103-hPI — имплантата нового поколения для инкапсуляции человеческих островков; запланировано исследование в Канаде. Оно опирается на завершённую первую фазу у человека (NCT06408311), в которой сообщалось о нефиброзной интеграции в ткани, васкуляризации и жизнеспособных островках в извлечённых имплантатах ENC-201. Это важно, так как инкапсуляция — один из путей клеточного замещения без иммуносупрессии, главного барьера для широкого применения. Эффективность (выработка инсулина у людей) пока не показана. Источник: [Encellin/BusinessWire через FinancialContent, 22.09.2026](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/bizwire-2026-9-22-encellin-partners-with-breakthrough-t1d-to-advance-immune-protected-cell-therapy-for-type-1-diabetes).

Источник: [MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2026/09/dreamed-wins-fda-clearance-for-cgm-driven-insulin-automation-tool-for-both-type-2-injection-regimens/).

### 4. Доступность и политика
В Сенате США внесён законопроект INSULIN Act of 2026, ограничивающий личные расходы пациента суммой $35 или 25% согласованной цены минимум по одному инсулину каждого типа и лекарственной формы (заявление ADA). Это только законопроект; перспективы принятия неопределённы. Источник: [ADA](https://diabetes.org/newsroom/press-releases/american-diabetes-associations-statement-introduction-insulin-act-2026).
