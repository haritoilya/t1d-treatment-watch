# T1D Treatment Weekly Report — 2026-11-06

**Scope note.** Weekly update against the 2026-06-24 baseline. Search coverage this run was limited: the search tool returned mostly aggregator/press-release snippets, and direct page fetches to several primary sources (BioSpace, GlobeNewswire, Breakthrough T1D) failed with network-resolution errors. Items below are therefore sourced from search-result summaries; primary documents were not independently opened. Dates were cross-checked where possible and uncertainties are flagged. The week was light on major news.

## Headline of the week
No single major breakthrough was identified. The most substantive items are (1) Sana Biotechnology's Q3 2026 update, which re-highlights long-term data on UP421 (hypoimmune islets without immunosuppression) and progress toward the SC451 IND, and (2) Breakthrough T1D's 5 Nov 2026 response to a federal request for information urging reclassification of deceased-donor islets as organs.

## 1. Cell & Regenerative Therapies

**Sana Biotechnology — Q3 2026 update (6 Nov 2026).** Sana reported Q3 2026 results, again citing a follow-on peer-reviewed NEJM letter (Uppsala clinicians; first announced July 2026) with ~14-month follow-up of UP421, a hypoimmune-edited primary islet product transplanted into one patient without immunosuppression; reported PET-MRI at weeks 12 and 52 showed islet cells at the forearm site. This continues the August 2025 NEJM report (12-week data) tracked in the baseline. Caveats: a single patient, a low dose designed for safety/function rather than glycemic benefit, and durability claims drawn mainly from company communications. Sana's stem-cell-derived follow-on, SC451, is guided to an IND "as early as 2026", and a Mayo Clinic collaboration is advancing. Sources: Sana Q3 2026 release (via BioSpace, 6 Nov 2026); Sana IR NEJM announcements.

**CRISPR Therapeutics — Q3 2026 update (2 Nov 2026).** CRISPR's business update describes CTX213, a gene-edited, deviceless beta-cell replacement candidate, as advancing toward the clinic after preclinical work, per search summary. This extends the company's earlier CTX211 programme (history in baseline). No clinical data were reported; the primary release could not be opened to verify timelines. Source: GlobeNewswire, 2 Nov 2026.

**Vertex zimislecel (VX-880) — status unchanged in available sources.** The latest retrievable information dates to Vertex's Q3 2025 summary: pivotal FORWARD enrollment complete, 83% of participants insulin-independent at Month 12, dosing temporarily paused, chronic immunosuppression still required, filing intended in 2026. I could not confirm whether the pause lifted or a filing occurred; this should be checked against Vertex's Q3 2026 release next week. Source: Vertex program update (2025) via search.

**Preclinical.** A 4 Nov 2026 bioRxiv preprint (not peer reviewed) in mice reported that a skin infection reduced allogeneic islet graft survival with nanotherapy-induced tolerance, while a single steroid dose improved survival. Interesting for infection-related graft loss but early and non-human. Source: bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.11.04.763192.

**Century Therapeutics CNTY-813.** Per a search summary, IND submission is expected Q4 2026 with initial clinical data in H2 2027 (company guidance, not independently verified).

## 2. Immunotherapy & Disease-Delay
No new trial readouts were found for October–early November 2026. Regulatory context: teplizumab received EC marketing authorisation in January 2026; Germany's G-BA amended its assessment on 6 Aug 2026 (indication: stage 2 T1D, age 8+); ADA 2026 real-world data suggested lower insulin doses in patients treated with teplizumab after progression to stage 3. COUR Pharma raised a $50M Series B (19 Jun 2026; Lumira, Roche Venture Fund, new investor Sanofi) for CNP-103 — older than the 7–10-day window but flagged as a possibly missed item. Sources: G-BA decision (6 Aug 2026); Clinical Trials Arena (ADA26); The Pharma Letter.

## 3. Devices & Insulin Delivery
No new AID/CGM clearances dated October–November 2026 were found; the index may lag. Background items: Diabeloop DBLG2 FDA clearance (Dec 2025; first AID algorithm cleared by FDA and CE-marked without mandatory meal announcements; Twiist integration targeted for 2027 launch); Tandem Control-IQ+ reportedly cleared for pregnancy (Apr 2026, secondary source — verify); Insulet began a fully closed-loop study in type 2 diabetes (May 2026), with a 510(k) planned for 2027. Sources: Barchart/Diabeloop; Pulse.bot (Tandem); Drug Delivery Business (Insulet).

## 4. Access, Affordability & Policy
**Deceased-donor islet classification (5 Nov 2026).** Breakthrough T1D filed a response to a federal RFI urging HHS to classify deceased-donor islets as organs, arguing that current classification limits US access. It cites CIT-07 results (87.5% with HbA1c <7.0% and no severe hypoglycemia at one year). Matters because regulatory classification gates availability of islet transplantation for patients now. Source: Breakthrough T1D, 5 Nov 2026 (via search summary).

**INSULIN Act of 2026.** Introduced in the House with ADA statement of 3 Sep 2026: $35 monthly cap for plans before 2028, then the lesser of $35 or 25% of net negotiated price. Current status unconfirmed. A June 2026 study found the Medicare insulin cap lowered out-of-pocket costs and increased use. Sources: ADA statement (Sep 2026); News-Medical (8 Jun 2026).

## 5. Asia & Emerging Research Centers
**Searched:** Deng Hongkui/Peking University; China T1D cell therapy trials (Chinese-language query); Japan iPSC islet programmes (Kyoto). **No new Asian developments dated within the last 7–10 days were found.** Status of key programmes:
- **Deng Hongkui / Peking University / Reprogenix Bioscience.** Autologous chemically induced iPSC-derived islets (Cell, 2024; ChiCTR2300072200): first patient insulin-independent from day 75 and one-year endpoints met. Reprogenix lists RGB-5088 with IND approval and Phase 1 started, Phase 2/3 anticipated 2026, BLA projected Oct 2027 (company conference listing; unverified). Single reported patient; follow-up publications could not be dated.
- **Shanghai Changzheng Hospital / CAS.** Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology report of a recipient insulin-free for 26 months (per search summary).
- **Kyoto University Hospital / Orizuru Therapeutics (Japan).** Allogeneic iPSC-derived islet sheet OZTx-410; investigator-initiated trial (jRCT2053240146), three patients aged 20–64 planned, first transplant performed, commercialisation targeted for the 2030s. No 2026 results found.
- NMPA registrations and RIKEN programmes: no new items surfaced.

## 1. Клеточная и регенеративная терапия

**Sana Biotechnology — отчёт за 3-й квартал 2026 г. (6 ноября 2026 г.).** Компания вновь сослалась на рецензируемое письмо в NEJM (клиницисты Уппсалы; впервые анонсировано в июле 2026 г.) с наблюдением около 14 месяцев за UP421 — гипоиммунно отредактированным продуктом из первичных островков, пересаженным одному пациенту без иммуносупрессии; по сообщению, ПЭТ-МРТ на 12-й и 52-й неделях выявила островковые клетки в области предплечья. Это продолжение публикации NEJM августа 2025 г. (данные за 12 недель), отслеживаемой в базовом отчёте. Оговорки: один пациент, низкая доза, рассчитанная на безопасность и функцию, а не на гликемический эффект, а утверждения о длительности основаны главным образом на сообщениях компании. Производный от стволовых клеток продукт SC451, по ориентиру компании, получит IND «уже в 2026 г.»; развивается сотрудничество с Mayo Clinic. Источники: релиз Sana за 3 кв. 2026 г. (BioSpace, 6.11.2026); публикации Sana IR.

**CRISPR Therapeutics — обновление за 3 кв. 2026 г. (2 ноября 2026 г.).** По поисковой сводке, CTX213 — генно-отредактированный кандидат для замещения бета-клеток без устройства — продвигается к клинике после доклинических работ. Это продолжение программы CTX211 (история — в базовом отчёте). Клинических данных не приведено; первичный релиз открыть не удалось. Источник: GlobeNewswire, 2.11.2026.

**Vertex zimislecel (VX-880) — в доступных источниках статус не изменился.** Последние доступные данные — сводка Vertex за 3 кв. 2025 г.: набор в pivotal-исследование FORWARD завершён, у 83% участников инсулиннезависимость к 12-му месяцу, дозирование временно приостановлено, требуется постоянная иммуносупрессия, подача заявки планировалась на 2026 г. ## 4. Доступность, стоимость и политика
**Классификация островков от посмертных доноров (5 ноября 2026 г.).** Breakthrough T1D направила ответ на федеральный запрос информации, призвав HHS классифицировать такие островки как органы, поскольку нынешняя классификация ограничивает доступ в США. Приведены данные CIT-07 (87,5% с HbA1c <7,0% без тяжёлой гипогликемии через год). Значимо, так как регуляторный статус определяет доступность трансплантации островков уже сейчас.

**INSULIN Act 2026.** Внесён в Палату представителей; заявление ADA от 3 сентября 2026 г.: лимит $35 в месяц для планов до 2028 г., далее — меньшее из $35 или 25% чистой согласованной цены. Текущий статус не подтверждён. Исследование июня 2026 г. показало, что лимит на инсулин в Medicare снизил расходы пациентов и повысил использование.
