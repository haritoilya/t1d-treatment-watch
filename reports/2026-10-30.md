# T1D Treatment Weekly Report - 2026-10-30

**Scope note.** Previous report on file: baseline of 2026-06-24 (no weekly reports in between are archived in this repository, so this run covers a gap of roughly four months at lower depth). Web search this run returned mostly previews, company guidance and older items; **no post-meeting EASD 2026 results, no October 2026 regulatory actions and no Asian primary publications from the last 7–10 days were retrievable.** Items below are stated at the confidence the sources support; anything not confirmed is labelled as such.

## Top line
No single confirmed breakthrough this week. The most decision-relevant items are (1) EASD 2026 (Milan, 28 Sep–2 Oct) featured a stem-cell-islet session and a Sana hypoimmune-islet symposium, but outcome data were not found; (2) several cell-therapy filings are scheduled for Q4 2026 (Century IND; Sana SC451 IND "later in 2026"; Vertex regulatory submissions "expected 2026") and none was confirmed filed in sources found.

## 1. Cell & regenerative therapies
- **Sana Biotechnology (HIP islets).** Sana announced (July 2026) a symposium at EASD on 2 Oct on hypoimmune-engineered allogeneic primary islets given to a T1D patient without immunosuppression (UP421, Uppsala University Hospital, investigator-sponsored). Its Q1 2026 report (11 May 2026) reported 14-month survival and function without immunosuppression and kept the goal of filing the SC451 (iPSC-derived HIP islet) IND and starting Phase 1 later in 2026; a Mayo Clinic collaboration was also announced. We found no confirmation that the IND has been filed or cleared. Single-patient, company-reported data. Continues baseline tracking of the HIP platform. Sources: Sana IR (Q1 2026), Sana EASD announcement (1 Jul 2026).
- **Century Therapeutics (CNTY-813).** Company guidance: IND submission in Q4 2026, initial clinical data in H2 2027; iPSC-derived islet replacement with immune-evasion design. Not yet confirmed as filed. Source: MyChesCo report.
- **Vertex (zimislecel).** Phase 1/2 data (NEJM, ADA 2025): 10 of 12 patients insulin-independent at ≥1 year, all in glycemic target range; requires chronic immunosuppression; encapsulated VX-264 was discontinued. Vertex has guided to regulatory submissions in 2026; **no 2026 BLA filing or acceptance was found in this run's sources**. Please verify against Vertex's Q3 2026 release.

## 2. Immunotherapy & disease delay
- **Teplizumab.** EU marketing authorisation granted in early January 2026; Germany's G-BA assessed it on 6 Aug 2026 (age ≥8, stage 2 T1D), finding an advantage in time to clinical onset. Real-world data at ADA 2026 (New Orleans): 84% without progression at 1 year, 57% at 2 years (observational, authors caution on limitations). Sanofi's observational study NCT07360080 is recruiting (updated Sept 2026). Extends the baseline's teplizumab history into reimbursement and real-world evidence. No new trial readouts found this week.

## 3. Devices & insulin delivery
- Nothing new dated October 2026 found. Most recent confirmed items: Tandem FDA clearance of Control-IQ+ for pregnancy (April 2026) and Diabeloop DBLG2 510(k) (Dec 2025). Separately, MannKind received a Breakthrough T1D grant (June 2026) for a first pediatric trial of inhaled insulin (Afrezza) in newly diagnosed youth.

## 4. Policy, access, affordability
- A US bill (H.R. 10227, companion to a Senate bill reported to have advanced out of HELP earlier in 2026) would cap commercial-plan insulin cost-sharing at the lower of $35/month or 25% of negotiated price, from plan years beginning 2028. Sourced from a single bill-tracking site; verify on Congress.gov. No other Sept/Oct 2026 access developments found.
- Early detection: the EU Commission hosted events on T1D early detection and screening (28–29 Sep 2026); the BR1DGE consortium presented at EASD. Informational; no policy decision found.

## 5. Funding & partnerships
- Only confirmed 2026 item: MannKind–Breakthrough T1D grant (June 2026, amount not found). No new financing rounds located.

## 6. Asia & Emerging Research Centers
Searched: Deng Hongkui/Peking University chemical reprogramming; Reprogenix; Japan iPSC islet (Kyoto); Chinese-language search for 2026 T1D cell-therapy trials. **No new Asian primary publication or trial announcement from the last 7–10 days was found.** Status of key programs:
- **Deng Hongkui / Reprogenix (China).** Basis remains the single-patient autologous chemically induced iPSC-islet case (Cell, 2024; one-year results: insulin independence from day 75, TIR 43% → 96%; patient was already immunosuppressed after liver transplant). Reprogenix lists RGB-5088 as lead with IND approval and Phase 1 started; a June 2025 conference listing projected Phase 2/3 in 2026 and a BLA in Oct 2027. Whether Phase 2/3 has begun is **unconfirmed**.
- **Kyoto University Hospital (Japan).** Investigator-initiated trial of allogeneic iPSC-derived islet cell sheets (OZTx-410; jRCT2053240146), 3 planned patients aged 20–64, first transplant performed in 2025, commercialisation targeted for the 2030s. No 2026 outcome data found; a July 2026 podcast with Daisuke Yabe discussed the trial without new numbers.
- NMPA registrations and other Chinese companies: nothing new surfaced in searches.
