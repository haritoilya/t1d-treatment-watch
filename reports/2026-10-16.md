## 1. Immunotherapy & Disease-Delay

**Immunocore — IMC-S118AI, first patient dosed (Oct 15, 2026).** Immunocore announced first-patient dosing in a Phase 1 trial of IMC-S118AI, described as its first tissue-specific autoimmune candidate, in people with T1D. The candidate is designed to protect beta cells from immune attack. It matters because it tests a tissue-targeted approach rather than broad immunosuppression, but this is a first-in-human safety stage with no efficacy data. *Source: company press release via GlobeNewswire/Manila Times, Oct 15, 2026 (full text not retrievable in this run).*

**SAB Biotherapeutics — SAB-142 Fast Track (Oct 13, 2026).** The FDA granted Fast Track designation to SAB-142, a human anti-thymocyte immunoglobulin made on a transchromosomic bovine platform, for new-onset Stage 3 T1D. The registrational Phase 2b SAFEGUARD study (participants within 100 days of Stage 3 diagnosis) is expected to complete enrollment in Q4 2026 with topline data in 2H 2027; an investigator-led Phase 3, PRISE-hATG, covers 100 days to two years post-diagnosis. Phase 1 (healthy volunteers, Jan 2025) met safety/pharmacodynamic goals. Fast Track speeds interaction with FDA but is not an efficacy finding. *Source: SAB Bio press release / ir.sab.bio, Oct 13, 2026 (company-reported).*

**Teplizumab, baricitinib, verapamil — status.** No new 2026 readouts were found. Teplizumab (Tzield) is reported as approved in Germany since January 2026 (Helmholtz Munich); the Phase 3 βETA PRESERVE trial in Stage 3 T1D (ages 1–25) is listed as ongoing. Eli Lilly's baricitinib Phase 3 trials BARICADE DELAY (Stage 1b/2) and BARICADE PRESERVE (new-onset) are running without reported results. Verapamil data (CLVer) date from earlier years. *Sources: Breakthrough T1D trial list (Jan 2026); Helmholtz Munich.*

## 2. Cell & Regenerative Therapies

**Reported University of Chicago islet-transplant trial (early Oct 2026) — unverified.** NBC News reported that a University of Chicago trial combining islet transplantation with an anti-rejection drug potentially cured all 12 participants; a separate explainer notes it was a small, single-site pilot with no control group, participants still require some immunosuppression, and data were first presented in March 2026. A follow-up search could not confirm the 12-participant figure or locate a publication (registry records found for Chicago islet studies list different enrollments). Treat as preliminary. *Source: NBC News, Oct 2026 (search summary only).*

**Vertex — zimislecel (VX-880).** No filing confirmation or Q2/Q3 2026 update was found. Last confirmed guidance (2025): global regulatory submissions planned for 2026 after pivotal enrollment/dosing of ~50 patients; Phase 1/2 data showed 10 of 12 participants insulin-free at one year, all on immunosuppression; VX-264 (encapsulated) was discontinued in March 2025. **Check Vertex's Q3 2026 earnings release for whether the filing occurred.** *Source: Vertex IR (2025); status for 2026 not confirmed.*

**Sana Biotechnology — UP421 / SC451.** Q2 2026 results (Aug 10, 2026) report UP421 (hypoimmune primary islets, Uppsala, no immunosuppression) with surviving, C-peptide-producing cells through 14 months, and an NEJM follow-up on durability. Sana presented "Hypoimmune islets" at EASD (Oct 2, 2026); presentation content was not found. SC451 (iPSC-derived hypoimmune islets) remains pre-clinical (GLP toxicology, manufacturing transfer); no IND clearance found. Mayo Clinic invested $25.0M. Financing is a near-term risk: Q4 2025 cash was $138.4M with runway guided into late 2026. *Sources: Sana IR Q2 2026; Sana EASD announcement (Jul 1, 2026).*

**Evotec — EASD 2026 posters.** Two posters on iPSC-based T1D therapy: immune-shielding with a kill-switch for use without immunosuppression, and scalable manufacturing of immune-shielded islet-like clusters. Preclinical-stage. *Source: Evotec EASD 2026 listing.*

## 4. Access, Affordability & Policy

**INSULIN Act of 2026 (U.S. House, Sept 3, 2026).** Five representatives introduced a bipartisan bill that, per the American Diabetes Association, would cap out-of-pocket insulin costs at $35/month; a companion Senate proposal would fund a five-year pilot for uninsured people. ADA polling: 72% of insulin users paid more than $35/month and ~40% more than $150. Only introduction was confirmed; no committee action found. *Sources: ADA statement; Endocrine Society.*

## 5. Asia & Emerging Research Centers

**Searched:** Deng Hongkui/Peking University; Reprogenix/NMPA; Shanghai/Beijing/Tianjin trials; Japan iPSC islet programs (Kyoto/Orizuru); PubMed-style queries for China/Japan. Asian news is moderate this week; no single dominant development.

**Peking University — Deng Hongkui (chemical reprogramming).** Continuation of the 2024 *Cell* case (25-year-old woman, autologous chemically induced iPSC-derived islets, insulin independence ~75 days post-transplant; two later patients reported similar within 75 days). A Breakthrough T1D CRC 2026 recap (Oct 2026) describes his lab working on AI-based scale-up of chemical reprogramming and strategies to avoid immunosuppression. Evidence remains a very small case series. *Sources: Breakthrough T1D CRC 2026 Day 3 recap; Cell (2024).*

**Reprogenix (Hangzhou) — RGB-5088.** Company materials state IND approval and an ongoing Phase 1, with Phase 2/3 anticipated in 2026 and a China BLA targeted for 2027. No 2026 NMPA announcement was found; the Phase 2/3 start is unconfirmed. *Source: BIO convention listings (2025–26 company statements).*

**Shanghai (CAS Center for Excellence in Molecular Cell Science / Changzheng Hospital) — E-islet.** Reported March 2026 in *The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology*: stem-cell-derived islets in T1D patients; sources conflict on sample size (three per Xinhua, six per China Daily). A related allogeneic regenerative islet trial (NCT06196996, 18 planned) lists completion in September 2026. *Sources: CAS (Mar 18, 2026); Xinhua/China Daily via secondary coverage.*

**Japan — Kyoto University Hospital / Orizuru Therapeutics (OZTx-410).** First allogeneic iPSC-derived islet-sheet transplant performed Feb 2025 (announced Apr 2025), safety-focused trial of three patients, commercialization targeted for the 2030s. No 2026 results found. *Source: Kyoto University Hospital / Jiji.*

SAB-142 — человеческий антитимоцитарный иммуноглобулин, получаемый на трансхромосомной бычьей платформе. Регистрационное исследование фазы 2b SAFEGUARD (в течение 100 дней после диагноза стадии 3) должно завершить набор в IV квартале 2026 г., топ-лайн данные — во 2-й половине 2027 г.; исследовательская фаза 3 PRISE-hATG охватывает период от 100 дней до двух лет после диагноза.
